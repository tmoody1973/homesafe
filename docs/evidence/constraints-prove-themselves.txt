HomeSafe — database constraints refusing bad data
Captured 2026-08-13 against cluster drying-gerbil (CockroachDB v26.2.5)

These are not tests that assert. These are the database refusing to store
data that would let HomeSafe mislead someone.

--- 1. A public record cannot exist without saying what it does NOT prove ---
Attempted: INSERT INTO public_event (...) with no caveat

  ERROR:  null value in column "caveat" violates not-null constraint

Why this matters: "a permit is not proof that a repair happened" is the
single most important caveat in the product. It is enforced by the schema,
not by anyone remembering to add it.

--- 2. An unknown issue category is rejected at write time ---
Attempted: INSERT ... event_category = 'not_a_real_category'

  ERROR:  failed to satisfy CHECK constraint (event_category IN
  ('heat_hot_water':::STRING, 'pest':::STRING, 'structural_safety':::STRING,
   'permit':::STRING, 'utilities':::STRING, 'sanitation':::STRING,
   'other':::STRING))

Why this matters: a typo in an ingestion adapter fails immediately rather
than producing a category that renders as a blank badge in the UI.

--- 3. Migrations are idempotent ---
  $ bun run migrate
  nothing to apply
